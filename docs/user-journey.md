# User journey

<!-- This file describes the intended user flow and the moments that matter. -->

## Scenario
Maya, a product manager, needs a short brief before a roadmap review. She has three approved research summaries but has not yet formed a recommendation.

| Stage | User action | Product response | Desired feeling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prepare | Selects a focused set of sources. | Confirms the source boundary and shows what is included. | In control |
| Ask | Chooses “summarize themes” and provides a question. | Restates the task and creates a draft. | Oriented |
| Review | Reads claims and opens their citations. | Shows source labels, evidence gaps, and conflicts. | Trusting but critical |
| Refine | Edits wording or flags a weak claim. | Preserves the user’s edits and invites verification. | Accountable |
| Decide | Copies the reviewed brief into her working document. | Reminds her that the final recommendation is hers. | Prepared |

## Key moments
- **Trust moment:** Can Maya locate the evidence for an important claim in seconds?
- **Recovery moment:** What happens when the sources do not answer her question?
- **Control moment:** Can Maya modify or reject output without fighting the interface?
