## The opportunity
Research synthesis is slow because evidence is distributed across multiple artifacts. The opportunity was not “add AI,” but to reduce assembly work while preserving the ability to verify claims.

## My product approach
1. Defined a bounded-input workflow rather than an open-ended chatbot.
2. Made citations and uncertainty first-class output requirements.
3. Scoped an MVP around draft creation and review, not autonomous recommendations.
4. Planned qualitative, rubric-based, and adversarial evaluation before expansion.

## Why the prototype is intentionally limited
The prototype is a conversation aid, not a product claim. It uses fictional, pre-written content so a reviewer can assess the interaction design without believing that an underlying model has been evaluated. The scope communicates a deliberate product trade-off: make provenance and human review visible before investing in automation, integrations, or scale.
